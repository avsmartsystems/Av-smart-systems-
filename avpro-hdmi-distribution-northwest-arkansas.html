Common questions

What is an HDMI matrix?

A box that lets any source, like a streamer, cable box or game console, show on any TV. All the gear can live in one closet.

Why not just buy cheap HDMI cables?

Long HDMI runs often fail with cheap cables. Professional cables and extenders keep 4K video stable.
