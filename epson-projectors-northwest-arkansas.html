Common questions

What is the difference between Home Cinema and Pro-Cinema?

Home Cinema models are built for living rooms and brighter spaces, with great value. Pro-Cinema models are aimed at dedicated theaters, with higher-end optics, deeper contrast and more placement flexibility.

Projector or big TV?

A projector shines at 100 inches and above and for a theater feel. A TV is brighter in daylight rooms. We help you decide for your space.
