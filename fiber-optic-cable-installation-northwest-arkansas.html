Common questions

Is this the same as fiber internet?

No. Fiber internet comes from your provider. We run fiber between your own buildings, for example from your network equipment at the house to a shop, barn or property entrance, so those spots get a fast, steady connection.

Why use fiber instead of network cable?

Standard network cable is limited to about 328 feet. Fiber goes much farther. It also does not carry electricity, so it avoids ground loops and helps keep surges from traveling between buildings.

Why not just use a wireless link?

Wireless can work for short, clear lines of sight. A buried fiber link gives you a steadier connection with no line-of-sight problems.

How much does a fiber run cost?

It depends on distance, the route and whether we trench or use existing conduit. Send the distance and layout in the form and we will quote it.

Do you install fiber internet from the provider?

No. Your internet provider installs its own service to your home. We handle the wiring and equipment on your side, between your buildings.

Do I need to call before digging?

Yes. Buried lines must be located before any digging. We plan that into every outdoor run.
