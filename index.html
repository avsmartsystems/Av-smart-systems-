Questions

Do you install Control4, Crestron or Savant?

Our focus is audio, video, WiFi, shades, cell signal and wiring. If your project needs a programmed whole-home control system, we will tell you up front.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Send your details with the form and we will reply by email with questions and a written quote. Larger projects get a design first.

What areas do you serve?

Bentonville, Rogers, Fayetteville, Springdale, Bella Vista and communities across Northwest Arkansas. Ask us about nearby areas.

Can you work with equipment I already own?

Often yes. We can reuse good equipment and only replace what is holding the system back.

How long does an installation take?

A TV mount is usually an hour or two, a sound system a few hours to a day, and larger projects are scheduled after the design is approved.

Do you work with builders?

Yes. We can coordinate pre-wire with your builder or general contractor so data jacks, speaker wire and TV locations are ready before drywall.
