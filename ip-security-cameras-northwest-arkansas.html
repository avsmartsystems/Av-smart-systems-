Common questions

How are IP cameras different from battery cameras?

IP cameras are wired for power and video, so they do not depend on batteries or WiFi strength. Many homeowners choose them for steadier recording and sharper video.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

Many professional systems, including Ubiquiti UniFi Protect, record locally with no required monthly recording fee. Optional features vary by brand, and we explain costs up front.

Can you put cameras on a shop or barn?

Yes. We extend network cable or a wireless link to outbuildings and mount cameras there.

Do you install Ring?

Yes. We also install Ring doorbells and cameras, and can pair them with a wired IP system for full coverage.
