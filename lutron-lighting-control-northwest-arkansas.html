Common questions

Caseta, RadioRA 2 or RadioRA 3?

Caseta suits simple upgrades. RadioRA 2 and RadioRA 3 are professional systems for larger homes and fuller control. We help pick the right tier.

Can Lutron go in an existing home?

Yes. Caseta is designed for retrofits, and RadioRA systems also work in existing homes.
