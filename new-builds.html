Common questions

When should pre-wire happen?

After framing and as the electrical rough-in starts, and before insulation and drywall. Call us during planning so locations are decided early.

Do you work with our electrician?

Yes. We handle the low-voltage cabling for data, TV, speakers and WiFi, and coordinate locations with your electrician so boxes and power line up.

What is a data jack?

A wall outlet with a network cable behind it, usually Cat6, that gives a wired internet connection for a TV, computer or WiFi access point.

Can you quote from floor plans?

Yes. Send a PDF of the plans, or tell us the room layout, and we will give you a written quote.

Can homeowners hire you if the builder does not include pre-wire?

Yes. Many homeowners add pre-wire as an upgrade. Tell us who your builder is and your timeline in the form.

How much does pre-wire cost?

It depends on the number of rooms and cable runs. Send your plans and we will give you a clear written price.
