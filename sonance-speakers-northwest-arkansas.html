Common questions

Why choose Sonance over regular in-wall speakers?

Sonance focuses on speakers designed to be installed in walls and ceilings, with trimless looks, matched speaker families and outdoor options. That tends to mean cleaner looks and even sound across a room.

Can speakers go in a home that is already built?

Often yes. We check attic and wall access during the quote and tell you what is possible.

Can the patio play the game while the living room plays music?

Yes. With an audio matrix or multi-zone amplifier, each zone can play a different source at its own volume.
