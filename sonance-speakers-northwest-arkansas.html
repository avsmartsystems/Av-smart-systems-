How a Sonance install works

Walk the home

We check room size, attic and wall access, and where you sit and entertain.

Design

A written layout with speaker locations, wire runs and a price before any work begins.

Install

We run wire, set each speaker flush and paint grilles to match.

Tune

We set up and tune the system and show you how it works.

We never plaster over speakers. Plaster-over designs hurt high-frequency clarity and cannot be serviced, so we use trimless and magnetic-grille options you can remove.

Existing home or new build?

In a new build, speaker wire is cheap to run before drywall, so our pre-wire plan is the best time to decide. In a home that is already built, we can often fish wire through attics and walls. We check access during the quote and tell you honestly what is possible.

Common questions

Why choose Sonance over regular in-wall speakers?

Sonance designs speakers specifically for walls and ceilings, with trimless and paintable looks, matched speaker families and outdoor options. That means a cleaner look and more even sound across the room.

How much do Sonance speakers cost?

Our Sonance theater packages start at about $4,829 in equipment at retail, with installation quoted after a walkthrough. Whole-home and patio systems are priced to the number of zones.

Can Sonance speakers go in a home that is already built?

Often yes. We check attic and wall access during the quote and tell you what is possible.

Can the patio play the game while the living room plays music?

Yes. With an audio matrix or multi-zone amplifier, each zone can play a different source at its own volume.

Will you paint the grilles to match?

Yes. We paint trim and grilles to match your wall and ceiling colors.
