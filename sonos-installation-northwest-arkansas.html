Common questions

Does Sonos need good WiFi?

Yes. Sonos plays over your home network, so we check and improve your WiFi when needed.

Sonos or Sonance?

Sonos is wireless and quick. Sonance is wired and hidden in the walls for a cleaner, more custom look. Many homes use both: Sonance for main areas and the patio, Sonos for extra rooms.
